Method & data

Precip type uses the Revised Bourgouin algorithm (Birk et al. 2021, Wea. Forecasting) on each member's wet-bulb profile; its four probabilities (rain, snow, freezing rain, sleet) split that member's QPF.

Snowfall = 3-hr QPF × snow share × snow-to-liquid ratio (Kuchera: warmest temperature surface–500 hPa), summed over the period.

Periods are rolling windows ending at the selected hour. Before the window fits in the run, the total since initialization is shown.
