Method & data

Precip type uses the Revised Bourgouin algorithm (Birk et al. 2021, Wea. Forecasting) on each member's wet-bulb profile; its four probabilities (rain, snow, freezing rain, sleet) split that member's QPF. Freezing rain is limited to a 2 m temperature of +1 °C or colder (sleet +3 °C), the warmer cases becoming rain. For GEFS only, where the model itself says at least 90 % of the precipitation is frozen, the freezing-rain share is also capped by its liquid fraction (100 − percent frozen precipitation); IFS ENS and GEPS publish no such field, so they get no cap. For accumulations the shares are averaged over each 3-hr step rather than taken at its end.

Snowfall = 3-hr QPF × snow share × snow-to-liquid ratio, summed over the period. The default is Kuchera (warmest temperature surface–500 mb, 12:1 baseline); the selector also offers a fixed 13:1, 12:1 or 10:1 applied to the snow liquid equivalent. Fixed ratios in the 12–13:1 range often verify better than 10:1 in the Northeast.

Freezing rain and sleet are the liquid-equivalent part of each member's QPF that the Bourgouin probabilities assign to that type. Freezing rain is not radial ice accretion (no wind, surface or icing-efficiency adjustment); sleet is liquid, not pellet depth.

Models. GEFS (31 members, 15 pressure levels), ECMWF IFS ENS (50 perturbed members, no control, 5 levels) and ECCC GEPS (21 members, 5 levels) are all resampled to the same 0.5° grid. The coarser IFS and GEPS profiles give the same dominant type as GEFS in about 97–98 % of wet cells but produce somewhat more freezing rain, so treat their freezing-rain fields as a little high. IFS and GEPS were not verified against ASOS reports.

Blend. The blend pools the members of the models that share the same initialization time (GEFS is matched to the newest 00/12Z cycle the others have) at the forecast hours all three provide. The default weights each model equally, so one GEFS member counts 1/31 of a third, an IFS member 1/50 and a GEPS member 1/21; “Equal by member” instead counts every member the same. Percentiles, probabilities, means, PMM and LPMM are all weighted, and the tooltip shows each model's own value next to the blend. A model that has not finished or is on an older run is left out and noted in the panel.

Statistics are computed per grid cell from the member totals (percentiles interpolate between ranked members, or between weighted ranks in the blend). PMM (Ebert 2001) keeps the ensemble-mean pattern but takes its amplitudes from all members pooled over the map; LPMM (Clark 2017; Snook et al.) does the same inside a neighborhood around each point.

Periods are rolling windows ending at the selected hour. Before the window fits in the run, the total since initialization is shown.

Hover (desktop) or tap (phone) the map for the ensemble breakdown at that point. Click to pin.
